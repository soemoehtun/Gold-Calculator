ရွှေတွက်နည်း — Gold Conversion
Convert between Myanmar gold weight units (Kyat, Pae, Yway) and MMK.
Conversion
1 ကျပ် (Kyat)= 16 ပဲ (Pae)
1 ပဲ (Pae)= 8 ရွှေး (Yway)
1 ကျပ်= 128 ရွှေး
Formula
Weight (Kyat) = MMK ÷ Gold Price
Operation Guide

ရွှေဝယ်မယ်: Enter your MMK amount and the current gold price per Kyat-tha to calculate how many Kyat, Pae, and Yway you can buy.

ရွှေပြန်ရောင်းမယ်: Enter the Kyat, Pae, and Yway weight along with the gold price to calculate the total MMK value.

Example (Buy): MMK = 1,000,000, Gold Price = 3,500,000 → Weight = 0.2857 Kyat = 0 Kyat 4 Pae 4.57 Yway

Example (Sell): 1 Kyat 2 Pae 3 Yway at 3,500,000 MMK → Value = 3,718,750 MMK
